## 4. Architectural Analysis: The "Almost" in Clustering

Clustering groups requirements by **semantic cohesion** (what belongs together). However, software architecture also requires classifying components into **architectural archetypes, layers, and dependency directions**:

### 1. Core Domain Services (Application Core)
- **Product Catalog & Search:** Core entity model for products, composite prepackages, units, and pricing rules.
- **Shopping Cart & Order Placement:** Manages order construction, shopping carts, blueprint cloning, and checksum validation.
- **Order Lifecycle & Cancellation:** Finite state machine managing status progression (Placed -> Assembled -> Delivering -> Completed/Cancelled).
- **Customer Identity & Account Management:** Customer entity, checksum verification, blacklist lookup, and address mapping.

### 2. Fulfilment & Operations
- **Fulfilment & Packing Operations:** Internal operational workflows for packing staff, paper bag labels, and duplicate invoice generation.
- **Delivery Logistics & Routing:** Itinerary route planning and field delivery confirmation.

### 3. Adapters & External Gateways (Hexagonal Architecture Ports)
- **SMS Ordering Gateway (Inbound Primary Adapter):** Receives SMS text messages, parses string syntax (`C... W... P...`), and delegates to Order Placement or Cancellation.
- **External Payment Integration (Outbound Secondary Adapter):** Listens for order packaging completion events and transmits expected-payment interface records to the external billing system.
- **Management Reporting (Outbound / Reporting Facet):** Scheduled nightly batch job querying order and staff metrics for management.
